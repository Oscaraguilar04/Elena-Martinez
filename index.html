About Elena

A neighbor who happens to know the paperwork.

Elena grew up around Sacramento weekends — farmers markets, bike-to-school streets, and the particular quiet of a Land Park evening. She works with families because the decision is never only about square footage. It is about where the kids will ride bikes, how long the commute really feels, and whether the house still works in five years.

Her approach is simple: explain the next step before you need it, tell you the unglamorous truth about a house, and stay reachable when the process gets noisy. You should feel like you can spend several Saturdays touring homes with her and still want to grab coffee after.

Favorite reset: a slow Sunday walk under the trees, then answering client texts from the porch.
